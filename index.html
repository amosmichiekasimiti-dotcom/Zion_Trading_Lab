📚 Zion AI Trading Lab - Complete Guide

🤖 AI SIGNAL ENGINE

Primary Signal

WAIT/RISE/FALL/EVEN/ODD/OVER/UNDER/MATCH - The main trading recommendation based on 5 conditions.
• Green = RISE/EVEN/OVER (Bullish)
• Red = FALL/ODD/UNDER (Bearish)
• Gray = WAIT (No trade)

Confidence Badge (0-100%)

• Green (60%+): Strong signal, safe to trade
• Yellow (30-59%): Weak signal, caution
• Red (<30%): Poor signal, avoid trading

5 Critical Conditions

Condition Checks Pass Criteria
Digit Distribution No digit dominates Max digit < 40%
Consecutive Pattern Streaks of even/odd 3+ consecutive same parity
Statistical Edge Even vs Odd imbalance >60% dominance
Price Action Price movement >0.5% change
Market Regime Volatility range 15-35% volatility

Metrics Explained

Trend Strength: % of ticks moving same direction (0-100%)
Volatility: Price fluctuation intensity (0-100%)
Reversion: Probability of trend reversal (0-100%)
Momentum: Price change % over last 10 ticks

💰 RISK MANAGEMENT PANEL

Kelly %: Optimal bet size using Kelly Criterion formula: (Win% × AvgWin - Loss% × AvgLoss) / AvgWin. Example: 33% = bet 33% of account per trade.

Max DD (Drawdown): Largest peak-to-trough decline in equity.
• Green (<15%): Safe | Yellow (15-25%): Caution | Red (>25%): Dangerous

Daily VaR: Maximum expected loss in 1 day at 95% confidence. Example: 5% = 5% of account at risk daily.

Position Size: Auto-calculated as Confidence ÷ 2 (max 0.50 lots).

Stop Loss/Take Profit: Dynamic stop = Volatility ÷ 2 (min 10 ticks). Fixed 2:1 reward/risk ratio.

Bot Runtime Limit: Slider from 5 minutes to 4 hours to auto-stop bot and prevent overtrading.

🎯 STEP-BY-STEP BOT RUNTIME DECISION

  1. Check AI Engine: Need 4+/5 conditions (80%+ confidence)
  2. Verify Risk: Kelly % >20%, Max DD <20%
  3. Confirm Session: London session (08-11 UTC) ideal
  4. Check Correlation: At least 1 other index confirming
  5. Review Backtest: Must say "RUN BOT"
  6. Sentiment: Avoid extreme greed (75+) or panic (25-)
  7. ML Agreement: Prediction should match AI signal
  8. Multi-TF: Score >70% for execution
  9. Set Duration:
    • Strong (80%+): 60-120 minutes
    • Moderate (60-79%): 30-60 minutes
    • Weak (<60%): Do not run
